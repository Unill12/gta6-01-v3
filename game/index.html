HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD moves, mouse aims, click shoots. Walk into a car and press E to steal it.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake/reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake. Ramming police and pedestrians raises your wanted level.

Wanted: stars bring police. At 3★ roadblocks appear, at 4★ a helicopter hunts you. Stay out of sight to cool down. If a cop corners you on foot, you get BUSTED.

Missions: follow the yellow marker. Complete all 12 to finish Vex's story and take the Bay from Silas Kane.

Money: missions, taxi fares, races, stunt jumps, contract hits and hidden packages all pay. Buy weapons at Ironworks Arms, cars at Vex Motors, and properties that pay you rent every day.

Save: your cash, mission, weapons, properties and packages save automatically.
